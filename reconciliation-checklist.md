# Monthly three-way reconciliation checklist

Use the statement's closing date for all three records. Work top to bottom.

Month: ____________  Prepared by: ____________  Reviewed by: ____________

## 1. Get everything in
- [ ] Trust bank statement for the full month, as a PDF, with cleared check images if the bank provides them
- [ ] Trust journal (register) printed or exported to the same date
- [ ] Client ledger listing: every client or matter with money in trust, to the same date

## 2. Bank to journal
- [ ] Every statement line ticked against the journal
- [ ] Deposits in the journal but not yet on the statement listed as deposits in transit
- [ ] Checks and transfers in the journal but not yet cleared listed as outstanding
- [ ] Anything on the statement but not in the journal (a bank charge, a wire you didn't record) found and fixed
- [ ] Adjusted bank balance equals the journal balance

## 3. Client ledgers
- [ ] Every journal line posted to exactly one client
- [ ] No client below zero at month end, or at any point in the month
- [ ] Closed matters at exactly zero
- [ ] Earned fees moved to operating only after they were billed

## 4. All three
- [ ] Adjusted bank, journal and client total are the same figure, to the cent
- [ ] Any difference traced to the record that is wrong, fixed there, and written down
- [ ] Reconciliation signed, dated and filed with the statement and the client listing

Check your state bar's trust account rules for how often to reconcile and how
long to keep these records.
